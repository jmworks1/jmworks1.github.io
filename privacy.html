Privacy Policy – Tiny Car Factory

Last updated: 14 October 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Tiny Car Factory (the "Game"), made by JMWorks ("we"), an independent developer based in Poland, collects and why. JMWorks is responsible for (the "controller" of) personal data processed through the Game. Google is responsible for data it processes for its own purposes, as explained below. The Game has no accounts of its own and does not ask for your name, email address or other contact details.

1. Your game progress

Your progress (cars, workers, money and settings) is stored on your device. A copy may be included in your phone's own Android backup if you have backups turned on. We do not receive or store your game progress on our servers.

Cloud save (Google Play Games). If you are signed in to Google Play Games, a copy of your progress is also saved in your Google Play Games account, so you can get it back after reinstalling the Game or on a new phone. Google stores this copy and shows it only to you and this Game. Signing in to Google Play Games also lets Google provide your Play Games profile (gamer name and avatar). See the Google Privacy Policy. You can delete your Play Games data in your Play Games profile settings.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game shows optional video ads that you choose to watch in exchange for in-game rewards. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's ad software may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID and app set ID,
  • your IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location,
  • information about how you interact with ads and the app (for example app launches, taps and video views),
  • diagnostic information about app and ad performance.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the Game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time with the Privacy choices button in the Game's settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings › Privacy › Ads, or Settings › Google › Ads).

Google's use of this data is described in Google's policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

3. Game statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the Game is played, for example how far players get in the tutorial, which cars they unlock, how many ads are watched and whether errors happen. This uses an app instance identifier and general device information (such as device model, operating system version, country and language). We only see grouped statistics and use them to improve the Game. We do not sell this data.

More information: Privacy and Security in Firebase.

4. Notifications

If you allow it, the Game schedules reminders on your phone (for example when your queue runs out or research finishes). These are created on your device; no information is sent to us for this.

5. Internet time check

To keep daily tasks fair, the Game asks a Google server for the current time. Only a standard web request is made; no personal information is attached to it by the Game.

6. Why we use your data (legal bases)

Under data protection laws such as the EU and UK GDPR, we rely on these legal bases:

  • Personalised ads: your consent, which the Game asks for through Google's consent form where the law requires it, such as in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).
  • Non-personalised or limited ads, ad measurement and fraud prevention: our legitimate interest in funding the free Game and keeping ads safe, within the choices you make in the consent form (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Game statistics: your consent where the law requires it, such as in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, given through the same consent form (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR); elsewhere, our legitimate interest in improving the Game (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Cloud save: providing the feature you choose to use (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Internet time check and replies to your emails: our legitimate interest in keeping the Game fair and answering you (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

You don't have to give us any data. If you don't consent, the Game still works and shows non-personalised or limited ads. We don't make decisions about you based solely on automated processing that have legal or similarly significant effects on you.

7. Who receives your data and transfers outside Europe

We don't sell your data. We share it only with Google, which provides the ads, game statistics, cloud save and time check. For ads and Play Games, Google acts as an independent controller under its own privacy policy; depending on your choices in the consent form, Google may also work with the other advertising technology providers listed there. For game statistics, Google processes data on our behalf.

Google may process data in the United States and other countries outside the EEA and the UK. For these transfers Google relies on safeguards recognised under the GDPR: the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework, under which Google LLC is certified, and the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses. More information: Google's data transfer frameworks.

8. Children

The Game is intended for players aged 16 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If you believe a child under 16 has used the Game and data has been collected, contact us and we will help remove it where we are able to.

9. Keeping data and deleting it

Game progress stays on your device until you uninstall the Game or clear its data. The cloud copy in Google Play Games stays until you delete it in your Play Games profile. Google Analytics deletes event-level and user-level game statistics automatically after the retention period we set (at most 14 months); grouped reports may be kept longer. Google keeps advertising data according to its own retention policy. Emails you send us are kept as long as needed to answer them and deal with any follow-up.

10. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access your personal data and get a copy of it,
  • have it corrected,
  • have it deleted,
  • restrict how it is used,
  • receive it in a structured, machine-readable format and have it sent to someone else (data portability),
  • object to our use of it based on legitimate interest,
  • withdraw your consent at any time with the Privacy choices button in the Game's settings, without affecting anything done before you withdrew it.

To use these rights, email us. Because the Game has no accounts, we usually can't tell which data is yours, so we may ask for details that help us find it. For data Google holds as an independent controller (ads and Play Games), you can also use Google's own tools, such as your phone's ad settings and your Google Account.

You can also complain to a data protection authority, for example in the country where you live or work. In Poland, this is the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO).

11. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the "last updated" date above.
